    Holt must improve validation MAE by at least 2% at each horizon to be selected; otherwise the forecast uses the last known price. Nominal 95% intervals are calibrated on an earlier period separate from the final test. The interval is omitted when calibration is insufficient. Actual coverage may be below 95%. A positive MAE gain means an improvement over the constant last price. The live ledger saves each issued endpoint with its original baseline and scores it when the corresponding actual arrives. Daily forecasts use spot series only, separate from futures and the heating-oil proxy. Horizons begin at the latest source date, not today.
